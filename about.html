About us

An international platform for digital services and connectivity

MJ HUB is an online digital services platform serving customers in supported markets worldwide. Customers can access SMS OTP and rental numbers across 90+ countries, reviewed digital product listings, Telegram verification and Stars services, social media growth packages, and MJHUB Wireless eSIM connectivity. Product availability, supported platforms, coverage, and plan features vary by country and service.

Create account Contact support

What we offer

  • MJ Logs: digital product and account listings reviewed by the MJ HUB team, with listing-level email and two-factor authentication security information.
  • MJ SMS: temporary SMS OTP numbers and longer-term rental numbers, including auto-renewal options, across 90+ countries. Supported services vary by country.
  • MJ Boosters: social media marketing packages for supported platforms, including selected visibility and engagement services.
  • Telegram services: Telegram blue-tick verification assistance and Telegram Stars services, subject to eligibility and platform requirements.
    • \n
  • MJHUB Wireless: eSIM connectivity with calling and texting features on eligible plans, subject to plan terms and coverage.
    • \n
  • Wallet and order management: customers can fund an account, place eligible orders, and track order progress.

How it works

  • Create an account and verify access
  • Fund your wallet
  • Choose a service and place an order
  • Track progress under Orders / History
  • Message support if something needs attention
