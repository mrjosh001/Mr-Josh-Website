About us

A digital marketplace built for speed and clarity

MJ Hub (www.mjhub.store) is an online platform where customers fund a wallet and buy digital services in one place: verification numbers, digital product listings, and social growth tools, with order history and human support.

Create account Contact support

What we offer

  • MJ Logs: digital product listings sold through our storefront. Where listings are user supplied, sellers list assets they own. Buyers purchase through MJ Hub’s order flow.
  • MJ SMS: temporary and rental numbers for verification and messaging, with status tracking in the dashboard.
  • MJ Boosters: social growth packages such as followers, likes, and views, ordered on a customer’s public link or profile.
  • Wallet: customers add funds, then pay for services without repeating bank details on every order.

How it works

  • Create an account and verify access
  • Fund your wallet
  • Choose a service and place an order
  • Track progress under Orders / History
  • Message support if something needs attention
