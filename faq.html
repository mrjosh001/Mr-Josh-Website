Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to frequently asked questions about our platform, services, and digital opportunities.

What is MJ HUB? ↓
MJ HUB is an all-in-one online digital platform offering premium social media engagement services through MJ Booster, secure buying and selling of verified logs via MJ LOGs, bulk SMS gateways, and digital numbering solutions. Enjoy top-quality services at competitive market rates.
A mass order — what does it mean? ↓
A mass order is an automation feature that helps you place several distinct service orders with different links all at once conveniently.
Are your Logs and digital services safe to order? ↓
Absolutely. Through MJ LOGs, you get access to securely prepared logs and system configurations where you will not face unexpected account lockouts.
What SMM services do you sell here? ↓
With MJ Booster services, you get the best of both worlds. Not only do we provide you with an amazing platform to easily manage your social media campaigns, but our services also help you optimize your campaigns for maximum results, target the right audience, and drive conversions.
Why do people use MJ HUB? ↓
Users trust MJ HUB for unbeatable service reliability, lightning-fast automated delivery, absolute privacy, and an extensive catalog covering MJ LOGs, MJ SMS gateways, and MJ Booster social growth.
How can I resell your services? ↓
You can contact our team to purchase a dedicated reseller panel. Connect via our API and start reselling our services directly to your own clients.
What steps should I take if additional information is needed for my account? ↓
Normally, extra information is provided directly in the form of pre-configured account credentials for sale through MJ LOGs. If a service requires deeper verification details outside our scope, our support team will guide you accordingly.
Are you spamming from your accounts? ↓
No, we do not spam from our accounts. We only use our accounts to grow them organically and ensure they are clean before listing them for sale via MJ LOGs.
For purchase of numbers, what country and service numbers are available? ↓
We provide active digital numbers spanning over 200 countries and supporting 300 unique web services, alongside our reliable MJ SMS delivery solutions.
What is the Drip-feed feature for? ↓
Through MJ Booster, you can build engagement on your chosen account at whatever speed you prefer. For example, if you want 1,000 likes on a post, you can choose to receive 100 likes per day over 10 days instead of getting them all instantly.
