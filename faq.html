Got questions?
We've got answers.

Everything you need to know about MJ Hub, our services, and how to get the most out of your account.

What exactly is MJ Hub? ↓
MJ HUB is an international digital services and connectivity platform for creators, businesses, marketers, and everyday users. Its services include SMS OTP and rental numbers in 90+ countries, team-reviewed digital product listings through MJ Logs, Telegram blue-tick verification assistance and Telegram Stars, social media growth services through MJ Boosters, and eSIM connectivity through MJHUB Wireless. Availability varies by service, country, platform, and plan.
What kind of accounts can I buy on MJ Logs? ↓
Our catalog includes Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), Gmail, Hotmail, Yahoo, Apple Music, Spotify, dating platform accounts, and many more. Each account comes with full login details, is quality tested before listing, and includes clear delivery instructions once you order.
Are the SMS numbers real or VOIP? ↓
All our numbers are real mobile lines, not VOIP. That means they work reliably with WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Google, and hundreds of other platforms that block virtual numbers. Rent a number, receive your OTP, and move on.
How fast is delivery? ↓
Most orders are delivered instantly to your dashboard. Account purchases appear within seconds. SMS verifications typically arrive within a minute. Social growth orders start processing immediately and complete within the timeframe listed on each service.
How do payments and the wallet work? ↓
You fund your MJ Hub wallet in either NGN or USD using your preferred payment method. Once funded, your balance is available instantly for any order. No monthly fees, no minimum spend, and no hidden charges.
What happens if an account I bought stops working? ↓
If you experience any issue with a login within the first 24 hours after purchase, contact our support team on WhatsApp or Telegram. We review each case fairly and issue replacements where valid. Please follow the login instructions carefully to avoid account triggers.
Do you offer social media growth for all platforms? ↓
Yes. MJ Boosters covers Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, X (Twitter), and other major platforms. We offer followers, likes, views, comments, and engagement packages. All services use high-retention delivery so your growth actually sticks.
What is drip-feed and when should I use it? ↓
Drip-feed lets you spread out delivery over time instead of receiving everything at once. For example, instead of getting 1,000 likes instantly, you can receive 100 per day for 10 days. This makes growth look more natural and helps avoid platform detection.
How many countries and services do your SMS numbers cover? ↓
Our SMS network spans over 200 countries and supports more than 300 different online services and platforms. From WhatsApp and Telegram to niche verification needs, we've got you covered.
Can I resell your services? ↓
Yes. We offer reseller access through our upcoming API. If you run your own panel or want to resell MJ Hub services under your brand, reach out to our team on WhatsApp or Telegram and we'll set you up with the right access.
Is my account information safe? ↓
Yes. Your login and wallet data are protected with industry-standard security. We never share your personal details with third parties, and all transactions happen through secure channels.
What if my question isn't answered here? ↓
Our support team is available 24/7 on WhatsApp and Telegram. Head over to the Support page or use the floating help button on any page to reach us. We usually reply within minutes.
