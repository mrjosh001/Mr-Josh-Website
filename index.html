Need SMS in Telegram?
Order without leaving the chat.

Use the official MJ SMS bot to fund a bot wallet and buy temporary numbers for verification codes. No website login required. Bot wallet is separate from your MJ Hub store wallet.

  • Order inside Telegram — no extra signup on the site
  • Temporary numbers for apps that send SMS codes
  • Type something like “USA WhatsApp” to see live prices before you pay
  • Number is sent in chat; check for the passcode in the bot. Cancel may be available after a short wait if no code arrives

How it works

Four steps inside Telegram.

1. Open the bot and press Start
2. Fund with /fund and an amount (e.g. 2k)
3. Type country + app (e.g. USA WhatsApp)
4. Buy, copy the number, and check for OTP in chat
