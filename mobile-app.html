Official install guide

Take MJ HUB
with you.

Add MJ HUB to your home screen for quick access to your account, orders and updates. Choose your device to get the right instructions.

Select Your Device

Which device are you installing MJ HUB on?

Install on iPhone / iPad

Use Safari for the best experience.

1
Open Safari

Visit www.mjhub.store in Safari. Avoid opening the link inside social media apps.

2
Tap the Share icon

Tap the square with the upward arrow in Safari.

3
Select Add to Home Screen

Scroll through the share options and choose Add to Home Screen.

4
Confirm the installation

Keep the name as MJ HUB and tap Add.

5
Launch MJ HUB

Find the MJ HUB icon on your Home Screen and open it.

If “Add to Home Screen” is missing, confirm that you opened the website in Safari, not Chrome or an in-app browser.

Install on Android

Chrome is recommended.

1
Open Google Chrome

Visit www.mjhub.store in Chrome.

2
Open the browser menu

Tap the three dots ⋮ in the top corner.

3
Choose Install app

Tap Install app or Add to Home screen, depending on your device.

4
Confirm and launch

Tap Install or Add. Open MJ HUB from your home screen or app drawer.

If the install prompt does not appear, use Chrome menu ⋮ → Install app / Add to Home screen.

MJ HUB · Secure access, right from your home screen.
This is an installable web app, not an App Store or Play Store download.
