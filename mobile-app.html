Install MJ HUB
on your phone.

Add MJ HUB to your home screen for quick, app-like access to your account and services. Choose your device below and follow the guide.

Which device are you using?

Select your phone to get the correct installation steps.

Install on Android

Use Google Chrome for the easiest installation. The exact wording can vary by phone and browser.

1
Open MJ HUB in Chrome

Visit www.mjhub.store in Google Chrome. If you opened this page inside another app, open the address in Chrome.

2
Tap the three-dot menu (⋮)

It is usually at the top-right corner of Chrome.

3
Choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”

If Chrome displays an install banner, you can tap that instead.

4
Confirm the installation

Tap Install or Add. Wait for the process to finish.

5
Open MJ HUB from your app list

Tap the MJ HUB icon on your home screen or in your app drawer, then sign in.

Your browser has not offered a one-tap install prompt. This does not mean installation is unavailable. Use Chrome’s three-dot menu and follow steps 2–4 above.

Samsung Internet: open its menu, choose “Add page to”, then “Home screen”. If you do not see an install option, try Google Chrome.

Install on iPhone or iPad

Apple’s Add to Home Screen method uses Safari. There is no separate App Store download for this web app.

1
Open Safari

Launch Safari and visit www.mjhub.store. If you are viewing this page in another app, copy the address and open it in Safari.

2
Tap the Share icon

Tap the square with an upward arrow. It is usually at the bottom of Safari, or near the address bar on some versions.

3
Tap “Add to Home Screen”

Scroll through the share sheet to find this option. If it is missing, check the available actions in the share sheet.

4
Tap “Add”

Keep the suggested MJ HUB name or edit it, then tap Add in the top-right corner.

5
Launch MJ HUB

Return to your Home Screen, tap the MJ HUB icon, and sign in to your account.

Important: complete these steps in Safari. The iPhone installation process is different from Android.
