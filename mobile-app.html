Install on iPhone

Use Safari. There is no App Store listing for this web app.

1
Open Safari

Visit www.mjhub.store in Safari (not Chrome or in-app browsers).

2
Tap Share

Tap the share icon (square with an upward arrow) at the bottom of Safari.

3
Add to Home Screen

Scroll the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.

4
Tap Add

Confirm the name MJ HUB, then tap Add in the top corner.

5
Open the app

From your Home Screen, tap the MJ HUB icon and sign in.

If you don’t see “Add to Home Screen”, open this page in Safari first.

Install on Android

Best results in Chrome.

1
Open Chrome

Visit www.mjhub.store in Google Chrome.

2
Install

Tap the install banner, or menu ⋮ → Install app / Add to Home screen.

3
Confirm

Tap Install or Add and wait a moment.

4
Open MJ HUB

Launch from your home screen or app drawer, then sign in.
