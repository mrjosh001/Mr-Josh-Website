Frequently asked questions

Important details before you continue.

How do I find my IMEI?

Open your phone dialler and enter *#06#. Copy the first 15-digit IMEI shown. Check every digit before reviewing your order.

Will every phone work?

No. Your phone must support eSIM and be network-unlocked. Compatibility can vary by model, region, and carrier status, so confirm before buying.

When will I receive my eSIM?

Delivery timing is not guaranteed on this page. Orders are intended for manual processing, and a confirmed delivery estimate should be provided once the order workflow is active.

Will I be charged when I tap Review order?

No. Review order only opens the summary. Payment integration is not active, so this page cannot charge you or confirm a completed purchase.
