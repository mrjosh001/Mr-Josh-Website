Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

MJ Hub is built for legitimate digital services: social growth tools, temporary verification numbers, and related catalog items offered for lawful use. This policy explains activities and content that are prohibited. It applies to every customer and visitor. Breaching this policy is a breach of our Terms of Service and can result in immediate account closure and, where relevant, referral to law-enforcement.

Strictly prohibited

You must not use MJ Hub to buy, sell, distribute, list, advertise, or facilitate any of the following:

  • Stolen, hacked, phished, brute-forced or otherwise unlawfully obtained accounts on any platform.
  • “Logs”, combo lists, credential dumps, or stealer output used for unauthorised access.
  • Account-takeover (ATO) services, cookie/session hijacking tools, or any bypass of multi-factor authentication.
  • Carding material: stolen credit cards, full-info (“fullz”), bank logins, CVV dumps, dump+pin sets.
  • Government-issued identity documents (real, forged, or templates) used for KYC fraud — passports, national IDs, driver’s licences, utility bills for that purpose.
  • Malware, ransomware, RATs, keyloggers, exploit kits, or instructions to create them.
  • Phishing kits, scam pages, fake-login templates, or services that impersonate real brands.
  • Hacked databases, leaked personal data, doxing material, or any content that exposes a third party’s private information without authorisation.
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any sexual content involving minors — zero tolerance.
  • Content that promotes or facilitates terrorism, organised violence, or human trafficking.
  • Sanctions evasion, money laundering, or transactions for designated persons / entities.
  • Counterfeit goods, pirated software, or any item that infringes a third party’s intellectual-property rights.
  • Engagement fraud at scale where the stated purpose is to deceive a third-party platform’s integrity systems in ways that violate law or that platform’s rules beyond ordinary marketing use.

Buyer responsibilities

  • Use delivered services in line with the destination platform’s terms of service.
  • Change recovery email, phone number and password immediately after delivery where credentials are involved.
  • Do not use purchased services to harass, defraud, impersonate or harm any person.
  • Do not resell delivered items in a way that breaches this policy.

Reporting violations

If you believe a listing or message on MJ Hub breaches this policy, report it via in-app support with as much detail as possible. We investigate reports and act as appropriate.

Enforcement

We may, at our sole discretion and without prior notice:

  • Remove a listing or message.
  • Reverse a transaction and refund the buyer where appropriate.
  • Suspend or permanently close the offending account.
  • Freeze wallet balances linked to the violation.
  • Cooperate with law-enforcement, including disclosing account information when required by valid legal process.

Updates

We update this policy as new abuse patterns emerge. The current version is always available at acceptable-use.html.
